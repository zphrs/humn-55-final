How to lead a social movement in the digital age:

So, you want to start a social movement. You've got the passion, you've got the motivation, and you've got a team. But how do you get your message out there? How do you get people to listen? How do you get people to care?

Social media platforms such as Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok are run by recommendation algorithms. These algorithms actively strive to keep users on the app for as long as it possibly can. They do this by running experiments millions of times every second on billions of users worldwide. The algorithm then learns what content keeps people - especially people similar to you - on the platform for longer, watching more ads.
